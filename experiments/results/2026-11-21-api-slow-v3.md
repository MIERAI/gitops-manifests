the delayed interface) past their default 1s timeout. Readiness took both api pods out of the Service and liveness

fast (0.002s) instead of slowly, so web-latency saw fast pages and never burned. Slowness became restarts. Fix: probe
timeouts 3s (readiness) and 5s x 6 (liveness) in every app (PR "Probes tolerate slowness"); rerun api-slow-v3 unchanged
after it is deployed.
