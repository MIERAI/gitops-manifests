## How to read this

- Budget left = (availability − objective) / (1 − objective), Pyrra's own figures; ⚠ < 25% (§6: promotions need a stated reason), ⛔ exhausted (feature promotions stop). While Prometheus holds less than 28 days, the window is what it holds: a young window makes a short blip a large share (§4 "a young SLO is oversensitive").
- Verified by: experiments without `skip:` whose `expect.fire` names the SLO, in whichever namespace they run (the rules are the same per environment). Last result: the newer of the results file and the lab's `slo_experiment_last_status`; a FAIL may be another expectation of the same experiment.
- Pages / tickets: firing episodes of the SLO's alerts (ErrorBudgetBurn, SLOMetricAbsent) by severity, not whether anyone was notified (`scripts/alert-report.py`); experiments run on the laptop count too.
- Gap kinds: untested (no experiment fires it), unverified (none passed), failing (the last run did not pass), stale (last pass older than `--stale`), runbook (anchor missing), budget, no-data, pages (> ~2/week).
