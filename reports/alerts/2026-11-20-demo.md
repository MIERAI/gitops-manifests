## How to read this

- experiment: a fault experiment marked the namespace (start → end + 30 min cooldown); expected / unexpected per its `expect`. Results files carry no cluster name: they are applied to the cluster given by `--results-cluster`.
- incident: an independent signal in the namespace (restart, running pod not ready, target down, failed job, new ReplicaSet = rollout) from 15 min before the start to 15 min after. A coincidence (e.g. a routine rollout) counts too: read the reason.
- noise: no experiment, no signal, and a noise signature (short, flapping, low traffic, absent right after Prometheus started). unknown: the data cannot tell — label it in `reports/alerts/labels.yaml`.
- Notified: routing and inhibition of the overlay at the commit in force; Alertmanager's own state (silences, delivery failures, repeat_interval) is not modelled — the speaker log is the check.
